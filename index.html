HELP & CONTROLS

Touch Screen (Mobile)

  • Left Thumb: Steering Left / Right
  • Right Thumb: GAS, BRAKE, and ERS. Brake reverses only at low speed.

Keyboard (Desktop)

  • Accelerate: W or Up Arrow
  • Reverse / Brake: S or Down Arrow
  • Steering: A / D or Left / Right Arrows
  • Boost: Shift or Spacebar

Gamepad

  • Left Stick: Steering
  • RT / A: Accelerate
  • LT / B: Brake / Reverse
  • LB / RB: ERS Boost

RACE STEWARDS & PENALTY SYSTEM

Passing checkpoints in order is mandatory. Brief run-off and recovery are allowed. Sustained off-track driving is reviewed, with three confirmed warnings before further breaches add a +2.0s penalty. A prolonged high-speed shortcut can add +3.0s.

TIMING KEY

Session best is the quickest sector in this session. Personal best improves your own prior split. +0.214s means time lost against the session benchmark. Purple, green, amber, and red alerts always include this plain-language label.
